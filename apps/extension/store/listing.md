# Store listing

Name: Cello

Short description: Fills job applications in your own browser. You click the employer's button.

## Long description

Cello fills job applications for you, in your own browser, on the form the employer hosts.

Open an application on Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby and click Fill. Cello reads the form, asks your Cello account for the answers it already has from your profile and the answers you saved, and fills what it knows. Anything it does not know is left empty and marked, so you can see what is yours to answer. It attaches your resume, and on a long question it can offer a draft that you read and change before you use it.

You press the employer's own Send button. Cello never presses it for you unless you have turned on Send for me.

Send for me is off until you turn it on in Cello and read what it does. When it is on, Cello can send some applications from this browser while your computer is on and the browser is open: only ones that are ready, that you chose yourself, and that fit your rule, within a daily limit you set. It never signs in, makes an account or answers a human check. When a form asks for any of those, or anything is not certain, it stops and hands the application to you with the reason. It counts an application as sent only when the site confirms it. Pause Cello stops it at once.

Cello never reads passwords, human check fields, or your answers to questions about gender, ethnicity, disability, veteran status, pay, work authorization or consent. It sends what it reads only to your own Cello account.

You need a Cello account. Cello is open source.
