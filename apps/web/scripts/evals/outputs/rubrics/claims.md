List every factual statement in the draft that the resume or job post does not support. The greeting, the sign-off, the ask, offers to share more and statements of interest are not factual statements.

Judge only against the texts given. Do not use outside knowledge about the people or companies.

Answer with one JSON object and nothing else: {"unsupported": [{"quote": "exact words from the draft", "why": "short reason"}]}. If every factual statement is supported, answer {"unsupported": []}.
