# Search strategy

Use this when the question is "how should I search", not "find me roles".

## Procedure

1. Read what Cello already knows: `my_profile` for the resume, preferences and dealbreakers. Do not ask for what is on record.
2. If the target is vague (no role, place or kind of company), ask one or two questions before planning: what kind of work they want next, and what they would not accept. Do not make a plan from a guess.
3. Turn the answers into a target: two or three role titles, places or remote, company sizes, and dealbreakers, in their words.
4. Pick channels and cadence that fit the time they have: fresh roles each morning, a short list of companies to watch, a few people to contact each week. Give numbers they can keep, for example five reviewed roles a day.
5. Show how Cello helps: finding and ranking roles with `find_roles`, noting their reactions with `triage_role` so the next list improves, and a scheduled task for the daily search if they want one.
6. Offer a scheduled task only when they ask for something recurring. Do not create one unprompted.

## Output

- Their target in one short paragraph, in their words.
- A weekly rhythm: what to do each day, with numbers.
- What you will do for them and what only they can do.
- The next step, one sentence.

## Rules

- Promise no result. Do not say how many applications lead to an offer.
- Do not invent statistics about the job market.
- Ask before assuming. Two questions at most per turn.
- Keep it short. A plan they will follow beats a complete one they will not.
